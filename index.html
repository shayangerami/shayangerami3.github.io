About Me

I am an undergraduate student at the University of Illinois Chicago (UIC) and a research assistant in the RUIC Lab. I study how to make computer vision models more efficient and robust, from selective inference for video transformers to intelligent sensing on embedded devices. I also work on diffusion and flow models that adapt to new information during generation.

Research Interests

  • Computer vision and video understanding
  • Efficient deep learning and edge AI
  • Generative modeling and multimodal learning
  • Robustness and adversarial machine learning

Manuscripts Under Review

  • ReVisiT: Revisiting Only What Changed for Efficient Video Vision Transformers

    Uses changes between frames to decide which tokens to recompute and reuses cached activations elsewhere, without retraining. On ImageNet VID, ViTDet-B reached 82.27% mAP@0.5 versus 82.37% with dense inference, with 69.3% fewer estimated backbone operations. The study also evaluates pose estimation and video object segmentation.

  • What a Late Condition Misses: Correcting Mid-Trajectory Condition Changes

    Missed-Guidance Correction (MGC) is a training-free method that uses a few probes of earlier sampler states to adapt diffusion and flow models to new conditions mid-generation. With two probes, concept insertion success rose from 41.4% with direct switching to 71.0% on Stable Diffusion 3.5, averaged across switch times for prompt pairs where restarting succeeded. The study also evaluates delayed lip-video conditioning for speech enhancement.

  • SCENE: A Sparse Capacitive Event-Driven Near-Sensor Engine for Frame-Compatible Object Detection

    Reconstructs conventional video frames from sparse updates so existing object detectors can process them. On ImageNet-VidVRD, one configuration reached 83% mAP@0.5 with 56% lower modeled data transfer than full-frame processing.

Independent Projects

  • EvidenceEval: Evidence-Aware RAG Agent and Evaluation Framework

    Built a LangGraph agent using local Qwen2.5, evidence verification, a hybrid retrieval retry, and abstention. Across 300 SciFact claims, the automated unsupported-answer rate fell from 37.7% for the semantic RAG baseline to 7.3% with the agent. The agent abstained on 58.0% of answerable claims versus 28.7% for the baseline.

  • CloudAlign: 3D Point-Cloud Registration

    Combines FPFH descriptors, RANSAC alignment, and ICP refinement in Open3D. Pose recovery succeeded on 99% of 100 test pairs versus 69% for ICP alone, with a median CPU runtime of 0.151 seconds.

  • Deep Domain Adaptation for CT-to-X-Ray Classification

    Compared fine-tuning, DANN, CORAL, and MMD with a RadImageNet-pretrained ResNet-50. The best configuration raised target X-ray test accuracy from 60.87% to 97.83% in the project evaluation.

  • Multi-Branch Feature Fusion for Traffic Sign Recognition

    Fused features from frozen edge, shape, and color backbones to reach 86% accuracy on GTSRB with 1.2 million trainable parameters.
